Episode 3: SEO and Billing — Optimization and monetization

After building core features, it was time to address visibility (SEO) and monetization (billing). Two critical aspects for a SaaS platform.

SEO Stripe Subscriptions

After developing core functionality, the next step was visibility (SEO) and monetization (billing). Both are essential for a SaaS product.

SEO optimization

The SEO audit revealed: private pages indexed, 302 redirects, incomplete robots.txt, missing Open Graph and Twitter Cards. We implemented optimized robots.txt, sitemap.xml, meta robots, canonical URLs, and Open Graph and Twitter Cards.

Stripe billing

Stripe was integrated with 6 plans: Free (5 applications/month), 15-day trial, 3 Starter/Pro plans, and a Full offer with 29% savings. Signup throttle was fixed so rate limiting applies only to POST requests.

Key learning: SEO and monetization should be considered from day one. Fixing them later is costlier and can hurt growth.
